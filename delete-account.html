Delete your Meetin account

Meetin, by the Meetin team. Last updated: 28 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

Open Meetin, go to Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted at once and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email meetinsupport27@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and your registered mobile number or Meetin ID. We may ask you to confirm from that number. We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Your name, profile details (bio, languages, topics, avatar), friends, blocks, notifications and push tokens. For listeners also the verification selfie and the PAN, bank and UPI details. Your mobile number is removed from the account, so you can sign up again later as a new user.

What is kept, and for how long

Records of payments, coin transactions, calls and listener payouts are kept under the name "Deleted user" for up to 8 years, because tax and accounting law requires it, and to handle refunds and fraud. They no longer show your name or number. Unused coins and unpaid earnings are lost when the account is deleted.

If you have a payout in progress, delete the account after it has been paid.
