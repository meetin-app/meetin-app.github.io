About Meetin

Meetin is a voice and video talk app operated by Meetin ("we", "us"). By creating an account or using the app you agree to these terms.

Who can use Meetin

You must be at least 18 years old and have a valid Indian mobile number. One person may hold one account, and you are responsible for activity on it.

Coins

Coins are a digital currency used only inside Meetin for paid calls and gifts. They are bought through Razorpay using UPI, cards or net banking. Coins have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money or transferred to another account, and expire only if your account is closed.

Paid calls

Calls with listeners are charged per minute at the rate shown in the app before the call (currently 10 coins per minute for audio and 30 coins per minute for video). The first 10 seconds of a call are free; after that each started minute is charged at its start. A call ends automatically when your coins run out.

Listeners

Listeners are verified women aged 18 or above who pass a live face check and PAN KYC. They earn a share of the coins spent on their calls and gifts, paid to their verified bank account or UPI after review. Earnings from fake calls, fraud or rule-breaking may be withheld.
