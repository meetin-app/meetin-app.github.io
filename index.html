How it works

Up and talking in two minutes.

Download and sign in with your mobile number and OTP.

Get coins from the Recharge screen. Pay securely by UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay.

Pick a listener who is online and speaks your language, and tap Audio or Video.

Talk. Coins are used per minute (audio 10 coins, video 30 coins). Rate the call when you are done.
