Child safety standards

Meetin (Android app "Meetin: Talk & Make Friends", developer "Meetin App"). Last updated: 11 November 2026

Meetin has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any sexual content or contact involving a minor. This page explains the standards we apply and how to report a concern.

Meetin is for adults only

Meetin is for people aged 18 and over. Everyone enters their birth year when they sign up, and the app does not let anyone younger than 18 create a profile. Listeners must also confirm they are 18 or older and pass a live selfie check before they can take calls. Accounts we believe belong to a minor are suspended.

What is not allowed

  • Any content, conversation or request that sexualises a minor, or that shares, asks for or links to child sexual abuse material.
  • Grooming: trying to build contact with a minor for sexual purposes, or asking someone to move to another app for that reason.
  • Sextortion, or threatening anyone to obtain sexual images or money.
  • Using Meetin while under 18, or helping a minor to use it.

Breaking these rules leads to an immediate and permanent ban.

How to report a concern

  • In the app: open the person's profile, tap Report and choose a reason. Under 18 and Abuse are among the options. You can also tap Block so they can no longer reach you.
  • By email: write to meetinsupport27@gmail.com with the subject "Child safety report", the person's name or Meetin ID and what happened. You do not need an account to report.

What we do with a report

Our team reviews every report. Child safety reports are handled first. When we find a violation we suspend the account at once, keep the information needed for an investigation, and ban the person permanently. We report child sexual abuse material and child exploitation to the relevant authorities, including India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and the police, and we cooperate with law enforcement requests, as required by Indian law (including the POCSO Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000).

Child safety contact

Our designated contact for child safety, including questions from Google Play and law enforcement, is the Meetin support team: meetinsupport27@gmail.com.
