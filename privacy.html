Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

What we collect

Your mobile number; profile details you enter (display name, birth year, gender, languages, topics, avatar); call history and coin transactions; and messages you send in the app. Listeners additionally provide a verification selfie, PAN details, a PAN card photo, and bank account or UPI details for payouts.

Phone number verification

You verify your mobile number with Truecaller, or with a one-time code we send by SMS through our provider StartMessaging (with Message Central as a backup), which receives only your number and the code for that purpose. If you use the Truecaller app, you confirm in Truecaller and we receive your verified number and your Truecaller first name. If you do not, the app downloaded from this website can verify you with a Truecaller missed call; it asks for phone and call log permissions only to detect and end that verification call. The Google Play version never asks for call log permissions and uses an SMS code instead. Meetin does not store or upload your call history or contacts. Truecaller's privacy policy applies to their part.

Calls

Voice and video calls connect directly between devices and are not recorded by Meetin.

Payments

In the app downloaded from this website, payments are processed by Razorpay. In the Google Play version, coins are bought through Google Play billing, and Google handles the payment. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank password. We keep the payment or order ID, amount and status to credit your coins and to handle refunds.

How we use your data

To run your account, connect calls, bill coins, pay listeners, prevent fraud, respond to support requests and keep the community safe.

Who can see it

Other users see only your display name, avatar, languages and topics. Verification selfies, PAN and bank details are visible only to you and authorised Meetin staff. We do not sell your data.

Storage and security

Data is stored with our cloud provider (Supabase) using encrypted connections and access rules so that each user can only read their own private data.

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account at the bottom; the account is deleted at once. You can also email meetinsupport27@gmail.com with your Meetin ID or registered mobile number and the subject "Delete my account", and we delete it within 7 days. Your profile, friends, verification documents and payout details are deleted. Payment, call and payout records are kept without your name for up to 8 years, as tax law requires.
